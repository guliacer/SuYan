# Bundled city index

Source: GeoNames cities15000 and admin1CodesASCII, downloaded November 13, 2026.

- https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities15000.zip
- https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/admin1CodesASCII.txt
- https://www.geonames.org/
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The source has been reduced to city names, aliases, regions, coordinates and population, sorted by population. No account or online geocoder is used. Run scripts/fetch-weather-cities.mjs to refresh the index.
